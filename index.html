Before you navigate

  • Directions are guidance only. Follow traffic laws and road signs. Real roads can differ from the map.
  • Do not touch the screen while driving. Mount the phone, or let a passenger operate it.
  • Routes and speed-camera data come from OpenStreetMap and can be incomplete or out of date.
  • Your location is used on this device to show your position and guide you. In this version it is not sent to a server.

Without accepting, you can still browse the map. Navigation stays off.
